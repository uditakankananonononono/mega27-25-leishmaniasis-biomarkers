# Leishmaniasis: proposed novelty test, not a result

**Current verdict:** No new named biological discovery, validated new method, or demonstrated same-task advantage. This is an unproven research direction, not a novelty claim or finished project.

## Falsifiable candidate contribution
A parasite- and lesion-stage-aware marker that transports to a clinically distinct cutaneous-leishmaniasis cohort, rather than a generic inflammatory skin signature.

## What would establish or refute it
Prespecify diagnosis and parasite strata, matched lesion/baseline tissue, negative inflammatory-disease controls, a new untouched cohort, and a published same-task comparator. Predeclare the endpoint, exclusion rules, analysis code and comparator before examining the held-out outcome; retain null and negative results. If the effect fails, the proposed contribution fails rather than becoming a post-hoc new marker.

## Why present evidence falls short
Retained discovery mixes cutaneous lesion and ex-vivo PBMC, whereas validation is visceral whole blood: not a single matched clinical task. P28 twenty-gene panel failed; P30/P35 two-gene recurrence is selected after an earlier outcome and is not a new discovery. The GSE125993 historical validation called eight HighAb-only specimens active disease; a source-strict, post-audit sensitivity still fails (19/43 signs; matched-null p=0.8926). Do not use GSE125991/125992 as independent from their parent GSE125993. Source lead: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE216638. A source lead is not proof of novelty, matching participants, or clinical utility.
