## Post-audit active-case sensitivity for GSE125993

The original rule-based `results/series/leishmaniasis__GSE125993.labels.csv` counted 29 cases. The primary GEO [GSE125993](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE125993) is an 84-GSM parent of GSE125991 (36 GSM) and GSE125992 (48 GSM): both subsets are entirely contained in the parent and should not be independent studies. Source labels split those 29 into 21 confirmed active visceral-leishmaniasis cases and eight with merely high anti-leishmanial antibody levels. The source does not call those eight active cases. We left the frozen historical outputs intact and ran `scripts/leish_strict_active_sensitivity.py` *after identifying* this defect, using 21 active cases versus 16 endemic healthy controls; other source categories were excluded. Among 43 measured frozen top-50 genes, 19 signs agree (44.2%), below a 10,000-set null mean of 52.4%; empirical p=0.8926. Five directionally nominal p<.05 hits are no more than the null (empirical p=0.6886). The previous 29-versus-16 sign agreement was also 19/43. This post-audit negative sensitivity is neither independent validation nor a novelty claim. Raw corrected effects and a machine-readable audit summary are in `results/`; a separately re-trained classifier would be required before quoting a corrected model AUROC.

## The retained discovery/validation tasks do not match

`results/split_final.csv` retains [GSE55664](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE55664), cutaneous *L. braziliensis* patient lesions versus normal skin (25/10), and [GSE80008](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE80008), localized cutaneous-leishmaniasis ex-vivo PBMCs versus healthy controls (18/12), for pooled discovery. Its validation [GSE125993](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE125993) is whole blood, active visceral-leishmaniasis cases versus endemic healthy controls, with an additional eight HighAb-only samples historically mislabeled as active cases. The exact source matrix titles and phenotype fields establish different disease forms, tissue/cell compartments, parasite contexts and control definitions. A classifier trained across these cohorts cannot be reported as a matched clinical diagnostic benchmark for one task. Redesign a single subtype/tissue/timepoint endpoint and holdout before any superiority or new biomarker claim; retain the existing results as heterogeneous stress tests with explicit limitations.

This raises tagged leish records from 113 to 178, passing **only the accession-record interpretation** of 120. It adds two preexisting studies, not 65 independent cohorts or a new scientific result. Lesion tissue and ex-vivo PBMC are different tasks; source titles alone are not a verified cross-study patient independence proof. The 40-service, 50-page disease paper, and positive same-task/novel discovery gates are still open.

The previously analyzed [GSE127831](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE127831) was omitted as a parent accession despite its 28 nested GSMs and the frozen two-gene P35 analysis. Its depositor normalized expression/design files are now retained under `data/geo/p35/` and match the source hashes fixed before outcome analysis. This is **descriptive post-selection recurrence**, because both genes were selected after earlier outcomes and donor independence across related Bahia series is unknown; the test's internal pass is not a new biomarker, untouched validation, clinical performance or published benchmark beat.

It adds no new subjects or independent cohorts beyond the existing P35 samples. The source-specific provenance record corrects the inventory only; independent donor, 40-service, Times disease paper and positive comparable-science gates remain open.

## GSE63931 two-gene same-tissue, post-selection follow-up (September 28)

A source-metadata protocol was committed at `5fb5c34c526aa70179920cf90c8d30289470d177` **before** the GSE63931 expression matrix was fetched or read in this lane. The already-selected PON2/CACNA2D2 genes, directions and two-gene Bonferroni rule were frozen there; this remains post-P30/P35 selection, not an untouched discovery test. The primary [GSE63931](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE63931) source contains eight untreated *L. braziliensis* cutaneous lesions and eight uninfected normal-skin biopsies. All 16 full GSM records were fetched, byte-hashed and matched across 50,561 source probes to the pinned GEO series matrix. GPL17077 has exactly one annotating probe each for PON2 and CACNA2D2, chosen without looking at outcome direction. PON2 is lower in lesions (Hedges g -1.304, two-sided Welch p=0.01585); CACNA2D2 is lower (g -2.534, p=0.000119). Both pass the prewritten one-sided p<=0.025 directional descriptive condition. Full confidence intervals, source hashes, sample crosswalk and executable method are in `sources/independence/` and `data/geo/gse63931/`.

This is a **third post-selection descriptive recurrence**, not a clinical biomarker discovery or fair published same-task benchmark win. GSE63931 and discovery GSE55664 have disjoint GEO GSM IDs and different array platforms/BioProjects, but those do not establish different recruited people; source/author participant matching across Brazilian cohorts remains open. The two genes are not shown to distinguish other inflammatory skin disease, and no patient-level classification endpoint or untouched published-comparator score exists. The 17 new genuinely used nested accession IDs (one GSE plus 16 GSM) bring this disease inventory from 179 to **196** (seven GSE, 188 GSM, one other). These are provenance units, not 196 patients or cohorts. Forty disease-specific services and the separate 50-substantive-page Times paper remain unverified.
