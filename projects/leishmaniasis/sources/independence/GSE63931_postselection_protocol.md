# GSE63931 candidate follow-up protocol, frozen before expression access

Date: September 28, 2026, about 10:40 IST. Local Git commit of this file must precede downloading/opening the GSE63931 matrix or individual probe outcomes in this lane. This is an explicitly **post-selection** follow-up of PON2 and CACNA2D2, chosen after the GSE214397 (P30) and GSE127831 (P35) results were seen. A pass cannot be upgraded to an untouched marker discovery or a fair published same-task benchmark win.

- GEO GSE63931: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE63931 . Full series SOFT, saved beside this file and pinned SHA-256 `b4df34eab61c5653605deae371d63457eeb434ed849eee95da4eb75e41841669`, states 8 untreated *L. braziliensis* skin lesions, 8 uninfected normal skin biopsies, platform GPL17077. The exact 16 GSM IDs have no overlap with retained GSE55664's 35 GEO IDs (series SOFT SHA-256 `02f68ebf7f694be3ffb50c80c0c6087af081fe9c39481adcbefeb1d66104f001`). Distinct GSMs, platform, BioProject and contacts do **not** prove participant-level independence, especially with Brazilian endemic cohorts; exact patient, recruitment site and collection-time overlap need source/author adjudication before claiming independent replication.
- Freeze sample inclusion: source titles starting `Cutaneous lesion` are cases (8); titles starting `Normal skin` are controls (8). No source record is to be dropped based on expression value. Check individual GEO title, organism, platform and source before analysis. If counts or phenotype mismatch, block and report, not relabel.
- Freeze genes and directions: PON2 down in lesions; CACNA2D2 down in lesions. Require exactly one unambiguous GPL17077 probe/gene mapping for each or predeclare a deterministic probe rule independent of case labels (highest across-all-samples mean). Avoid choosing whichever probe gives the desired direction. If absent/unmappable, mark not measured; no replacement candidates.
- Effect: normalized series matrix (pin its fetched SHA-256), map platform gene IDs with source annotation, compute Hedges g lesion-minus-control, Welch two-sided p and 95% CI. Descriptive two-gene support requires both measured with negative effect and both one-sided directional p <=0.025 after Bonferroni over the two fixed genes (equivalently two-sided p <=0.025 with negative direction, conservatively). No clinical classifier/AUC is implied. All 16 source samples stay in analysis; no post-hoc batch exclusion.
- Preserve complete results and failures. Compare same-tissue/parasite interpretation; do not pool with ex-vivo PBMC or visceral blood. A positive descriptive result still requires donor independence, a genuinely untouched cohort, negative inflammatory skin controls and a published same-task locked comparator before discovery credit. Existing 2024 multi-dataset secondary analyses already use GSE63931/GSE55664, so a reanalysis of those cannot be labeled independent new prior-art evidence.
