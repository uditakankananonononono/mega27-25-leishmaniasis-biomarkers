# Source collision: GSE63931 is not literature-untouched

Checked September 28, 2026. The 2024 Scientific Reports publication "Determination of key hub genes in Leishmaniasis as potential ..." explicitly selected GSE43880, GSE55664 and GSE63931 for a combined cutaneous-lesion analysis. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-73779-w . It performed differential-expression/network analyses and ROC analysis on GSE55664, discussing CXCL10 and GBP1 as hub genes; it does not establish that PON2 or CACNA2D2 were tested/ruled out. Thus our GSE63931 post-selection two-gene recurrence is neither a new untouched literature cohort nor a fair benchmark comparison to that paper's different genes/endpoint. Do not report it as a new marker. Published reuse says nothing definitive about whether the actual patient identities in the three GEO datasets overlap.

The source [GSE43880](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE43880) is a further same-region skin-lesion dataset (10 *L. braziliensis* lesions from Corte de Pedra and two North American healthy-skin controls), 12 samples on GPL10558. Its GSM IDs are disjoint from GSE55664/GSE63931, but GSE55664's lesions were also collected at Corte de Pedra and include two North American normal controls; without donor IDs, a repeated source specimen or participant is possible. GSE43880 is not an independent validation just because its GSMs differ, and the tiny two-person control arm impairs inference. No expression from GSE43880 was opened in this audit. Source series SOFT retained as `GSE43880.series.soft.txt` (SHA-256 `8acba6d8d7c45d5d82adaa39a1a978f10028bd0da778ec36cf38047a31e5e7a2`).

Next qualifying test should be on a patient-disjoint lesion cohort or new clinical task, with endpoint, sample unit, assay, comparator and metric registered before outcomes. Any repeated selected-gene follow-up is exploratory, even if its within-cohort p-value is small.
